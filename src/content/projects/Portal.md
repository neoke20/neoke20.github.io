## About the project

The employee portal was created after we asked ourselves a simple question: How can we make sharing information and resources between employees easier? With this in mind, we created a platform where all necessary information related to company policies and procedures is centralized. Later on, we also added a way for teams to share know-how about their jobs so new employees could be more independent in their work and learn on the job.

Finally, during the last iteration of updates, we added ways for employees to know who is or will not be in the office, what company events are happening on that day, and if there are any internal surveys they have not answered yet.
